COGNITIVE ECOLOGY / PRIVATE DEVELOPMENT
Φ

A.C.C.E.S.S.

An ecology of persistent AI entities, each with its own memory, values and evolving internal state. The project brings cognition, collective coordination and interaction with a personal Linux environment into one long-term research and engineering programme.

Research areas: memory and retrieval, neurochemical state models, decision-making, shared knowledge, cybersecurity and biometrics. The public notebook supplies a separate, reproducible model of isolation, delivery and stability.
